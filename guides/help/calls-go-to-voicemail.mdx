---
title: "My calls still go to voicemail"
description: "Why forwarded calls can land in your old voicemail instead of reaching your agent, and how to fix each cause."
---

You turned on forwarding, but callers still hear your old voicemail.
This almost always means the call never left your phone company.
Work through the checks below in order.

## First, find where the call stops

Call your business number from a different phone.
Then open **Calls** in the dashboard.

| What you see | What it means |
| - | - |
| No new call in **Calls** | The call never reached Larynth. The cause is at your phone company or on your phone. Keep reading. |
| A new call in **Calls** | Forwarding works. Check which agent answers the number, on the **Phone numbers** page. |

You can also watch the call arrive.
Open **Phone numbers**, open your number, and next to **Forward your existing number** select **Open**.
The **Test it.** section shows the call as soon as it reaches us.

## Your phone company's voicemail answers first

If you forward only missed calls, your phone company decides what happens to a call you do not answer.
It sends the call to voicemail unless missed-call forwarding is set for that exact situation.

Check that each situation is covered:

- **No answer.** You did not pick up.
- **Busy.** You were on another call.
- **Phone off or out of signal.** The call could not reach your phone.

On T-Mobile these are three separate codes.
If you set only "no answer", a call that arrives while the phone is off still goes to voicemail.
On Verizon wireless one code, `*71`, covers busy and no answer together.
AT&T does not publish wireless codes for missed calls, so set them in the phone's call settings.

The codes are in [Forward your calls: carrier by carrier](/guides/help/forward-by-carrier).

<Tip>
  To rule this out quickly, forward all calls for a minute and call again.
  If the agent answers now, the missed-call settings are the problem.
</Tip>

## iPhone Live Voicemail

Live Voicemail is voicemail that runs on the iPhone itself.
If it picks up a missed call before your phone company forwards it, the caller never reaches your agent.

To turn it off:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    On the iPhone, open **Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Phone settings">
    Tap **Apps**, then tap **Phone**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn it off">
    Turn off **Live Voicemail**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Also check **Silence Unknown Callers** in the same place.
Apple says that when it is on, unknown numbers go directly to Live Voicemail without ringing.
Most new customers are unknown numbers.

## Ring time

A missed call forwards only after your phone has rung for a while.
If the ring time is long, callers may hang up before the agent answers.

| Phone company | Ring time before a missed call forwards |
| - | - |
| T-Mobile | You choose it, from 5 up to 30 seconds. |
| Verizon wireless | 3 to 6 rings. Verizon does not list a way to change it. |
| AT&T wireless | "A few rings". AT&T does not list a way to change it. |
| RingCentral | You choose it with **Ring for** in the call handling settings. |
| AT&T and Verizon landlines | A preset number of rings, set by the phone company. |

On T-Mobile, dial `**61*1`, your Larynth number, `**`, the seconds, then `#`.
For example, `**61*14155550100**15#` forwards after 15 seconds.

If you cannot change the ring time and it does not suit you, forward all calls instead.

## Reset and start again

Old forwarding settings can get in the way of new ones.
Clear them, then set forwarding again from the start.

| Phone company | How to clear |
| - | - |
| T-Mobile | Dial `##004#`. T-Mobile describes this as resetting call forwarding to default. |
| Verizon wireless | Dial `*73`. The confirmation can sound like a busy signal. |
| AT&T wireless | Dial `#21#` and wait three seconds for the tone. This clears "all calls" forwarding. |

After a reset, missed calls go to your phone company's voicemail again until you set forwarding once more.

## Landlines

On AT&T and Verizon landlines, forwarding turns on only when the confirmation call is answered.
If your Larynth number did not answer that call, forwarding never started.
Check that the number is **Active** and a published agent answers it, then dial the code again.

Forwarding for busy or unanswered calls on these landlines is set by the phone company.
Call them and ask for "call forwarding busy line and don't answer" to your Larynth number.
