---
title: "Your first real calls: what to check"
description: "A short routine for the first days after you go live: listen, check summaries, fix answers and confirm your safety nets."
---

Your first real callers will ask things you did not plan for.
Spend a few minutes on each of the first calls.
Most fixes take one edit.

Before real calls, try the agent yourself.
See [Test calls](/guides/test-in-browser) and [Go live and verification](/guides/go-live-and-verification).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Listen to the first calls">
    Open **Calls** and select a call.
    Play the recording and read the conversation next to it.
    Check that the agent greeted the caller well, answered correctly and ended the call cleanly.
    Listen to at least your first three calls.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check that the summary arrived">
    Each answered call gets a summary on the call page, usually within 30 seconds.
    If you turned on summaries by email, push, Slack or Telegram, check that one arrived too.
    If it did not, see [Where your call summaries and alerts go](/guides/help/where-summaries-go).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fix one wrong answer">
    When the agent gets a fact wrong or doesn't know it, add the right answer to its knowledge.
    Go to **Knowledge** and open the knowledge base the agent uses.
    Choose **Add a source** ▸ **Typed answers**, then select **New answer**.
    Write the question the way callers ask it, and the answer you want given.
    Select **Save answers**.
    Your agents can use the answer in about a minute.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer the questions callers asked">
    Open the knowledge base and go to **Questions to answer**.
    It lists questions callers asked that no source could answer.
    Select **Add an answer** for each one that matters, or **Dismiss** it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rehearse tricky calls">
    Practice calls let you test the agent against callers you describe, in text, without a phone.
    Nothing is booked or sent during a practice call.
    Open the agent, then choose **Practice calls** from the agent menu.
    Select **Add starter calls**, or **Write your own**, then **Run all**.
    Run them again after each change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Practice calls don't support flow agents yet.
For a flow agent, use a test call instead.

## Check your safety nets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check that forwarding works">
    If you forward your existing number to Larynth, prove it once.
    Go to **Phone numbers**, open the number's menu and choose **Forwarding steps**.
    Call your business number from another phone.
    The page shows the call arriving and confirms **Forwarding works**.
    The **Forwarding works** step on **Go live** turns done after the first forwarded call.
    For your carrier's settings, see [Forward your calls, carrier by carrier](/guides/help/forward-by-carrier).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a backup number">
    A backup number catches calls if the agent can't pick up, for example during an outage or while the agent is paused.
    Open the number in **Phone numbers**.
    Under **If the agent can't pick up**, turn on **Transfer to a person** and enter the number.
    Select **Save routing**.
    Without one, callers hear a short apology and the call ends.
    If your balance runs out, calls are not answered and the backup number is not used either.
    Top up before you run low; we email you at $10 and at $2 left.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signs to act on

| You notice | What to do |
| - | - |
| The agent says it doesn't know | Add a typed answer, or answer it under **Questions to answer**. |
| The agent gives an old fact | Edit the typed answer, or update the page or file it came from. |
| Callers ask for a person | Check your transfer settings. See [Handoff to a person](/concepts/handoff). |
| Calls show **Not billed** | Short calls and likely spam are free. See [Billing](/concepts/billing). |
| No calls arrive at all | Check forwarding at your carrier and that the number routes to a published agent. |
