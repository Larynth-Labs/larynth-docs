---
title: "Before you release a number or close your account"
description: "Turn off forwarding first, so callers never reach a dead line."
---

If your business number forwards to Larynth, your callers depend on that forwarding.
Release the Larynth number or close your workspace while forwarding is still on, and callers reach a line that no longer answers.
Do these steps in order.

## 1. Turn off forwarding at your phone company

Turn off call forwarding on your business number first.
Do it with your phone company or phone system, the same way you turned it on.
See [Forward your number](/guides/forward-your-number).

Then call your business number from another phone.
It should ring your own phone, not your agent.

<Warning>
  If you release the Larynth number while forwarding is still on, callers hear that the number is not in service.
</Warning>

## 2. Set a backup number while you switch

A backup number catches calls that still reach your Larynth number while you switch, if your agent can't answer them.

If you only want a break, you don't need to release anything.
Open the agent, click **Pause agent**, and set a backup number on the Larynth number.
Calls then ring your backup phone.

To set it, go to **Phone numbers**, open the number, and edit **If the agent can't pick up**.
Turn on **Transfer to a person**, enter a 10-digit US number in **Transfer to**, and click **Save routing**.

Without a backup number, callers hear a short apology and the call ends.
If your balance runs out, calls are not answered and the backup number is not used either, so keep credit on the account while you switch.
See [Send calls to a person](/guides/help/send-calls-to-a-person) for when the backup number is used.

## 3. Release a number

Releasing a number stops your agent answering it and stops its monthly charge.

- Callers who dial it hear that the number is not in service.
- You may not be able to get the number back.
- The current month is not refunded.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the number">
    Go to **Phone numbers**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Release it">
    Open the number's actions menu and choose **Release number**.
    You can also open the number and choose **Release number** from **More actions**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Type the number to confirm, then click **Release number**.
    You may be asked to sign in again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only an Owner or Admin can release a number.
A number used by a campaign, or set as your opt-out line, can't be released until you remove it from the campaign or choose another opt-out line.

## 4. Close your workspace

Closing your workspace closes it for everyone.
When it runs, agents stop answering, numbers are released and the team loses access.
Only the Owner can do this.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a copy of your data (optional)">
    Go to **Settings** ▸ **Your data** and request an export first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request deletion">
    Go to **Settings** ▸ **Workspace** and click **Delete workspace…**.
    Read the dialog, then click **Request deletion**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the review">
    Our team reviews every deletion before it runs, usually within a few days.
    You can follow the request under **Settings** ▸ **Your data**, and cancel it until the work starts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Consent and opt-out records stay for 4 years, because the law requires it.

## What happens to your credit

Unused balance is handled as described in our [Terms of service](https://larynth.com/terms).
In short, the Terms say:

- Paid credit is not refunded when you close your account, except where the law requires it.
- If you close your account before a price change takes effect, the unused part of your paid credit is refunded.
- Free and promotional credit is never refunded.

Read the Terms for the full wording.
For questions about a balance, email [support@larynth.com](mailto:support@larynth.com).
