The dashboard page "Get your line ready" has two parts. The first gets you ready to answer real calls. The second, verification, opens outbound calling.

## Inbound checklist

Your agent can answer calls as soon as a number routes to a published agent. The checklist covers the rest:

| Step | What it means |
| - | - |
| Business details | Your business name and time zone. |
| Agent published | A published agent answers your calls. |
| Test call made | You heard your agent once before customers did. |
| Number ready | You bought a number or forward your existing one. |
| Hours set | Your open hours, or around the clock. |
| Transfer line set | A person to pass urgent calls to. |
| Disclosures reviewed | You checked what callers hear at the start. |
| Forwarding works | Shown only if you forward from another number. Done once a forwarded call has arrived. |

See [Get a number](/guides/get-a-number) and [Test calls](/guides/test-in-browser).

## Verification

Outbound calling opens after our verification partner confirms who you are. Inbound calls do not need verification.

You choose how to verify.

| | As an individual | As a business |
| - | - | - |
| Steps | Identity check | Identity check, business details, tax letter, callback number |
| Limits | Lower | Higher |
| List uploads | No | Yes |
| Marketing calls | No | On request |

The identity check is a photo ID and a selfie. It runs in a separate window hosted by our verification partner. You agree to the check before it opens, and you need your photo ID and a camera.

### Business details

The business path asks for:

- legal name, trade name (optional), EIN, state of registration, address, website, industry, and how you will use calls;
- the IRS letter that shows your EIN (CP 575 or 147C), as PDF, JPG or PNG up to 10 MB;
- a callback number.

The details must match your public business registration. Larynth stores only the last four digits of your EIN. The letter is encrypted, and only our review team can open it. If everything matches, approval is automatic. Otherwise our team reviews it, usually within one business day.

Businesses are re-checked every 6 months. Outbound pauses if a re-check expires, so update your details when you are asked.

### Callback number check

The callback number is a US phone number for your business that can take a call right now. Larynth places one short call to read you a six-digit code, and you enter it in the dashboard. The call is free. You can request it once every 10 minutes and 3 times a day, and you get 5 tries per code.

Forwarding to Larynth works automatically. If your business line forwards to a Larynth number, the call can be verified without a code.

A Larynth number can't be the callback number. Use your own business line.

### Limits by level

These limits are in force while you stay in good standing.

| Level | Daily spend | Calls at once | List upload | Marketing calls |
| - | - | - | - | - |
| Not verified | $5 test credit only, no outbound | 5 | No | No |
| Individual, first 30 days | $250 a day | 5 | No | No |
| Individual, after 30 days | No daily cap | 10 | No | No |
| Business, first 30 days | $1,000 a day | 20 | Yes | On request |
| Business, after 30 days | No daily cap | 50 | Yes | On request |

Need more? Ask [support](/support). Requests for higher limits are reviewed the same day, at no extra charge.

### Marketing calls

Marketing calls need approval. Verified businesses can request it from the same page. You show how people agree to receive marketing calls, with a web address of the sign-up form or an uploaded document, and a description of how consent is collected. Information calls work without this. Requests are reviewed within one business day.

### Verification status

Your page shows one of: not started, in progress, in review, approved, rejected, suspended or re-check due. A rejection shows a reason, and you can correct the details and try again.

## What else outbound needs

Verification is one gate. Outbound also needs a paid top-up, the current outbound terms accepted, and every check in [Outbound and campaigns](/guides/outbound-and-campaigns).
