Larynth gives your business an AI phone agent. It answers calls, makes calls you have consent for, hands a call to a person when it should, and sends a summary after every call.

You can set it up in the dashboard, or build on it with the API and SDKs. Both use the same agents, numbers and calls.

## How it fits together

1. An **agent** holds the instructions, voice and language, and what happens after the call. See [Agents](/concepts/agents).
2. A **phone number** sends calls to an agent. See [Phone numbers](/concepts/phone-numbers).
3. A **call** is one conversation, inbound, outbound or a browser test. See [Calls](/concepts/calls).
4. After each call, Larynth writes a **summary** and can extract the details you ask for. See [Summaries and extraction](/concepts/summaries).

## Price in one line

$0.10 per minute, with the phone line included. Each started minute is billed. Spam and calls under 10 seconds are free. Read [Billing](/concepts/billing) for the details.

## Availability

Larynth runs in regions (US, EU and APAC) and works with local phone numbers in each region.
