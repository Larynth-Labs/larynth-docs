<Warning>
  Larynth's checks help you follow calling rules. They do not make a call lawful on their own, and Larynth does not promise compliance. You are the caller. Your legal responsibility stays with you. Ask your own lawyer about your situation.
</Warning>

## Checks before an outbound call

Every outbound call, from a campaign or from `POST /v1/calls`, goes through these gates before it dials:

| Check | What it does |
| - | - |
| Verified account | The person or business behind the account is verified before it can place outbound calls. Unverified accounts can only receive calls. See [Go live and verification](/guides/go-live-and-verification). |
| Consent on file | The contact needs a consent record: how and when they opted in, and what they agreed to. No record, no call. |
| Do-Not-Call scrub | The number is checked against your Do-Not-Call list and the Larynth opt-out list. |
| Calling hours | The contact's state and local time are checked. Outside the allowed window the call waits. |
| AI disclosure | The agent says it is an AI assistant at the start of the call and names the business it calls for. |
| Recording notice | Callers hear a recording notice. |

Outbound calls to emergency numbers (911) are blocked.

## When a call is refused

A refused call returns an error with a stable `code` that names the rule, for example `dial.window.closed`. When the refusal is about calling hours, the error includes `retry_at`, the next moment the call may be allowed.

`GET /v1/compliance/decisions` lists every pre-dial decision, with the rule, the result and the time. Use it to see why a contact was not dialed.

## Consent

Record consent with `POST /v1/compliance/consents`. A record needs:

- the number (`e164`) and the `kind`: `express` or `express_written`
- when it was granted (`granted_at`) and how it was captured (`source`)
- evidence: an `evidence_ref` or `evidence_text`
- a scope: the whole account, or one campaign

Consent records are kept and cannot be deleted by you.

## Opt-outs and Do-Not-Call

- `POST /v1/compliance/revocations` records that someone withdrew consent. The number is added to your internal Do-Not-Call list.
- `POST /v1/compliance/dnc` adds a number to your internal list directly.
- Larynth keeps its own opt-out list, fed by opt-outs on calls, and applies it to every account.
- The agent can end a call and record an opt-out with the `opt_out` tool.

## What stays with you

- You follow the laws that apply to your calls.
- You bring your own Do-Not-Call registrations for outbound campaigns.
- You only call people who gave the consent your calls need.
- You tell your own staff that calls are recorded.
- You review the terms and the acceptable use policy for your situation.

See [Outbound with consent](/guides/outbound-with-consent) for the working steps.
