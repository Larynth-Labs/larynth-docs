## Price

| What | Price |
| - | - |
| Calls | $0.10 per minute, all-in, with the phone line included |
| Phone number | $2 per month |

Calls are billed per started minute. A call of 61 seconds is billed as two minutes. The price is the same for inbound, outbound and browser calls, and for both agent modes.

## What is free

- Spam calls that Larynth flags.
- Calls that last under 10 seconds.
- Calls to the opt-out line of a marketing campaign.
- $5 of free credit for new accounts, for browser and inbound test calls.

## What you're billed for

| Call or charge | What you pay |
| - | - |
| A call your agent answers or makes | $0.10 per started minute |
| A call under 10 seconds | Nothing |
| A call Larynth flags as likely spam | Nothing |
| A call from a caller you blocked | Nothing. The call is turned away. |
| A call while your agent is paused, sent to your transfer line | Nothing |
| A browser test call | $0.10 per started minute, from your free credit first |
| Time you or your team talk to a caller after a transfer | $0.03 per started minute, instead of the agent rate |
| A number set to ring another phone, with no agent on the call | The **Forwarded calls** rate under **Billing** ▸ **Rates** |
| A phone number | $2 per month, taken from your balance |

Spam calls are still answered.
They are just not billed.
The 10-second rule counts for every call, test calls included.

<Tip>
To block a caller, open the call and choose **Block this caller** from the more actions menu.
Their later calls are turned away and not billed.
</Tip>

### See why a call was free

In **Calls**, a free call shows **Not billed** in the price column, with the reason underneath.

| Reason shown | Meaning |
| - | - |
| under 10 s | The call lasted under 10 seconds. |
| spam | Larynth flagged the call as likely spam. |
| blocked caller | The caller is on your block list. |
| agent paused | The agent was paused, so the call went to your transfer line. |

Open the call to see the receipt, **What this call cost**, with the same reason in a sentence.
To list only free calls, use the **Billing** filter and choose **Not billed**.

### No surprises

- There are no overages. Calls draw from your prepaid balance, and nothing is charged after the fact.
- There is no auto top-up. Your card is charged only when you top up or buy a pack yourself.
- Bundles are one-time packs. They never renew.
- Number fees come from your balance, never from your card.

### Spending limits

You can set two optional ceilings in **Billing** ▸ **Overview** ▸ **Spending limits**.
Both are off until you set them.

| Limit | What it does |
| - | - |
| **Daily cap** | A ceiling on one day's spending. |
| **Monthly budget** | A ceiling on one month's spending. |

When a limit is reached, new outbound, API, campaign and test calls stop.
Calls in progress finish.
Inbound calls are still answered, unless you turn on **Also stop answering inbound calls**.
Larynth alerts you as you near each limit, at 80% and 100% by default.
Limits reset at midnight in your workspace's time zone.
Owners and Admins can change limits.

### Launch price

If a launch price applies to your workspace, the billing page shows it and the date it ends.

## Prepaid credit

You add credit to your account and calls draw it down.

- Credit is prepaid and non-refundable, except where the law requires a refund.
- Credit does not expire.
- Free credit is spent first.
- Outbound calling needs verification and a paid top-up. Free credit cannot be used for outbound calls. See [Go live and verification](/help/go-live-and-verification).
- Buying a phone number needs a paid top-up too. Free credit does not cover number rental.

While a call is in progress, Larynth holds an amount against your balance. When the call ends, the held amount is replaced by the real charge.

When credit runs out, campaigns pause and new inbound calls can be refused.

## Add credit

You add credit in the dashboard, on the billing page. Each top-up is a one-time purchase on a secure checkout page. When you finish, you return to the billing page, which shows the result. Tax and receipts are handled at checkout, and each payment keeps a receipt link.

- There is no auto top-up. Larynth never charges your card on its own.
- The card you pay with is saved for later top-ups, so repeat top-ups take fewer steps.
- Larynth emails you when your credit falls to $10 and again at $2, and the dashboard shows a banner. Each warning is sent once until you next add credit.
- Bundles, when on offer, are one-time packs bought the same way. They do not renew by themselves. Bundle credit pays call-minute charges only.

## See what you were charged

| Endpoint | Returns |
| - | - |
| `GET /v1/billing/balance` | Available funds, free credit, bundle credit, the amount held for calls in progress, and your low-balance threshold. |
| `GET /v1/billing/ledger` | Every charge and top-up, newest first. |
| `GET /v1/billing/payments` | Your payments, with tax, card and a receipt link. |
| `GET /v1/billing/rates` | The rates in force. |
| `GET /v1/billing/rate-card` | Your prices, the free rules and any launch price on your workspace. |

Amounts in the API are in micro-USD: 1,000,000 equals $1. A call's final price is `price_micro`, and the minutes billed are in `billable_ms`.

## Refunds

Prepaid credit is non-refundable, except where the law requires it. Ask [support](/help/support) if something looks wrong on a bill.

## Availability

Billing is in US dollars. Larynth phone numbers are US and Canadian, local or toll-free, at the same monthly price.
