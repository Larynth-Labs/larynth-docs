## Price

| What | Price |
| - | - |
| Calls | $0.10 per minute, all-in, with the phone line included |
| Phone number | $2 per month |

Calls are billed per started minute. A call of 61 seconds is billed as two minutes. The price is the same for inbound, outbound and browser calls, and for both agent modes.

## What is free

- Spam calls that Larynth flags.
- Calls that last under 10 seconds.
- Calls to the opt-out line of a marketing campaign.
- $5 of free credit for new accounts, for browser and inbound test calls.

## Prepaid credit

You add credit to your account and calls draw it down.

- Credit is prepaid and non-refundable, except where the law requires a refund.
- Credit does not expire.
- Free credit is spent first.
- Outbound calling needs verification and a paid top-up. Free credit cannot be used for outbound calls. See [Go live and verification](/guides/go-live-and-verification).
- Buying a phone number needs a paid top-up too. Free credit does not cover number rental.

While a call is in progress, Larynth holds an amount against your balance. When the call ends, the held amount is replaced by the real charge.

When credit runs out, campaigns pause and new inbound calls can be refused.

## Add credit

You add credit in the dashboard, on the billing page. Each top-up is a one-time purchase on a secure checkout page. When you finish, you return to the billing page, which shows the result. Tax and receipts are handled at checkout, and each payment keeps a receipt link.

- There is no auto top-up. Larynth never charges your card on its own.
- The card you pay with is saved for later top-ups, so repeat top-ups take fewer steps.
- Larynth emails you when your credit falls to $10 and again at $2, and the dashboard shows a banner. Each warning is sent once until you next add credit.
- Bundles, when on offer, are one-time packs bought the same way. They do not renew by themselves. Bundle credit pays call-minute charges only.

## See what you were charged

| Endpoint | Returns |
| - | - |
| `GET /v1/billing/balance` | Available funds, free credit, bundle credit, the amount held for calls in progress, and your low-balance threshold. |
| `GET /v1/billing/ledger` | Every charge and top-up, newest first. |
| `GET /v1/billing/payments` | Your payments, with tax, card and a receipt link. |
| `GET /v1/billing/rates` | The rates in force. |
| `GET /v1/billing/rate-card` | Your prices, the free rules and any launch price on your workspace. |

Amounts in the API are in micro-USD: 1,000,000 equals $1. A call's final price is `price_micro`, and the minutes billed are in `billable_ms`.

## Refunds

Prepaid credit is non-refundable, except where the law requires it. Ask [support](/support) if something looks wrong on a bill.

## Availability

Billing is in US dollars. Larynth phone numbers are US and Canadian, local or toll-free, at the same monthly price.
