## Contact us

- Email [support@larynth.com](mailto:support@larynth.com).
- Use the in-app chat in the [dashboard](https://app.larynth.com).

Support is by email and in-app chat for every customer.

## What to include

- The call ID, if the question is about a call. You can find it in the dashboard or in the API response.
- The time of the call and the number involved.
- What you expected and what happened.

Do not send API keys or webhook secrets. If you shared one by mistake, rotate it.

## Security reports

Email [support@larynth.com](mailto:support@larynth.com).

## Service status

Check [status.larynth.com](https://status.larynth.com) for current service status.
